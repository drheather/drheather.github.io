Laney Learns to Love

A tender picture book about how love grows through presence, connection, and everyday moments between a baby and her caregivers.

Written for little ones ages 0–5 and the grown-ups who love them, Laney Learns to Love blends lyrical storytelling with developmental wisdom.

About the Series

The Laney Learns series helps children and caregivers grow together through stories rooted in attachment, emotional development, music, movement, and mindful connection.

Stories

Warm, lyrical books that support emotional growth and everyday connection.

Songs

Gentle lullabies and playful music to extend each story into family life.

Caregiver Tools

Reflection prompts, connection tips, and practical supports for grown-ups.

For Grown-Ups

Laney Learns is created not only for children, but also for the adults holding, guiding, soothing, and growing alongside them.

Each resource is designed to offer warmth, reassurance, and practical insight for real-life caregiving—not perfection.

About Dr. Heather

Dr. Heather Mangelsdorf is a developmental psychologist, educator, musician, author, and parent. Through Laney Learns, she creates story-rich resources that help families grow together with wisdom, warmth, and wonder.
