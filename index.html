Books

Stories that grow with your child—and your heart.

The Laney Learns series is a collection of books, songs, and caregiver resources designed to help children and grown-ups grow together through connection, play, and everyday moments.

Inspired by developmental psychology, attachment theory, and real family life, each book focuses on a single emotional or developmental skill—from learning to trust and rest to navigating big feelings, friendship, curiosity, and resilience.

Laney Learns follows Laney Lou from birth through age five, with stories organized into age-based collections that grow alongside children and the adults who love them.

The Connection Collection
(0–1 Years)

Gentle, rhythmic stories that nurture love, trust, rest, sensory discovery, and secure attachment during baby's first year.

The Expression Collection
(1–2 Years)

Supporting toddlers as they learn to communicate, help, try, and explore their growing independence.

The Feelings Collection
(2–3 Years)

Exploring big emotions, early friendships, self-expression, and imaginative play.

The Shine Collection
(3–4 Years)

Building mindfulness, confidence, gratitude, empathy, and a growing sense of self.

The Discovery Collection
(4–5 Years)

Encouraging curiosity, problem-solving, self-regulation, and social understanding.

About

About Dr. Heather

Hi, I'm Dr. Heather.

I'm a developmental psychologist, educator, musician, author, and parent passionate about helping children and caregivers grow together with wisdom, warmth, and wonder.

With a Ph.D. in psychology and more than 15 years of experience as a researcher, professor, and learning designer, I've spent my career exploring how people learn, connect, and thrive.

But becoming a parent changed everything.

It invited me to live what I had studied—to slow down, tune in, and rediscover the quiet power of human connection.

It also awakened something I hadn't fully made space for before: my creative voice. Parenthood reminded me that I'm not just a scientist—I'm a singer, a storyteller, and a performer. I wanted to model authenticity for my child by living it myself. In doing so, I reconnected with the creativity and joy that now shape everything I create.

Today, I write the Laney Learns series—story-rich, developmentally grounded books that help children and caregivers navigate emotions, build connection, and find wonder in everyday moments.

Drawing on developmental psychology, early childhood education, and the learning sciences, I create books, songs, and resources that are both research-informed and deeply human.

At the heart of my work is a simple belief: children grow best in relationships. Learning is relational, playful, and multisensory. And the space where research meets real life is where meaningful growth begins.

Whether I'm writing about a baby's first smile, a toddler's big feelings, or the everyday moments that shape family life, my goal is always the same:

To nurture connection, creativity, and compassion—for the humans raising humans in a changing world.
